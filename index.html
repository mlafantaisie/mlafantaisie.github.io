The Warriors of Old

A single-player RPG game where death is not the end of your story.

Overview

You awaken as a new hero in the strange dark fantasy world of Sunderaeth, discovering it is overrun with undead and a plague upon the land known as the Blight. Every death is not the end for you, but a link to another time and place. Can you find the root of the Blight and restore Sunderaeth, or will you fall to the evils and be trapped for eternity?

Features

The game has a number of core systems designed to make your trip through Sunderaeth more interesting.

Death Loops

Artifacts and Crystals

Find and unlock secret artifacts forgotten to time and gain divine powers from mysterious crystals that seem to be able to bind the blight.
